# Changelog

New, changed or removed features, one line each, brief and direct. The section for a release becomes the release notes.

## 1.1.0

- New: Desert ambient light, off by default: in daytime missions on desert planets, Ambient light stays off, as the bright sand made the picture too bright. Turning the option on keeps the effect there. It applies at once, also from the pause menu.
- Changed: Shadow distance now covers every time of day and every area lighting of the planets. Before, only one lighting set per planet got the longer distance, so most missions kept the game distance whatever the option said.
- Changed: Enhanced Visuals loads after Integrated Third Person, which replaces the render config too.

## 1.0.2

- New: Enhanced Textures, an optional download beside this release: game textures at twice the size, upscaled by an AI model. Import the zip into Obsidian like any other mod.

## 1.0.1

- Changed: Render scale no longer draws glass, such as the shield on the ship, over the objects behind it.
- New: A thumbnail for Obsidian.

## 1.0.0

- New: Ambient light, in the manner of the Ambient Light shader for ReShade: deeper dark tones and a wide, soft glow around lit areas, Subtle or Strong.
- New: Ambient occlusion, a pass the game ships but never runs, now on with a new shader: darker light where surfaces meet, Subtle or Strong, on ambient light or on all daylight.
- New: Sharpening after anti-aliasing, Normal or Strong.
- New: Shadow resolution: the sun shadow map at 4096 or 8192, past the 2048 of the Graphics menu.
- New: Shadow distance: sun shadows reach 35 m or 60 m from the camera instead of about 20 m.
- New: Shadow edges: Soft and Softer sun shadow edges, at every shadow resolution.
- New: Render scale: the scene at 1.5 or 2 times the screen resolution, scaled down before the HUD.
- New: Model detail distance: models keep the most detailed version two or four times farther from the camera.
- New: Screen dust: the dust that drifts across the picture on most planets is off by default, with an option to bring it back.
- New: F9 switches between Enhanced Visuals and the original look of the game, to compare.
