# Changelog

New, changed or removed features, one line each, brief and direct. The section for a release becomes the release notes.

## 1.0.2

- New: Enhanced Textures, an optional download beside this release: the game's textures at twice the size, upscaled by an AI model. Import the zip into Obsidian like any other mod.

## 1.0.1

- Changed: Render scale no longer draws glass, such as the ship's shield, over the objects behind it.
- New: A thumbnail for Obsidian.

## 1.0.0

- New: Ambient light, in the manner of ReShade's Ambient Light: deeper dark tones and a wide, soft glow around lit areas, Subtle or Strong.
- New: Ambient occlusion, a pass the game ships but never runs, now on with a new shader: darker light where surfaces meet, Subtle or Strong, on ambient light or on all daylight.
- New: Sharpening after anti-aliasing, Normal or Strong.
- New: Shadow resolution: the sun's shadow map at 4096 or 8192, past the 2048 of the Graphics menu.
- New: Shadow distance: sun shadows reach 35 m or 60 m from the camera instead of about 20 m.
- New: Shadow edges: Soft and Softer sun shadow edges, at every shadow resolution.
- New: Render scale: the scene at 1.5 or 2 times the screen resolution, scaled down before the HUD.
- New: Model detail distance: models keep their most detailed version two or four times farther from the camera.
- New: Screen dust: the dust that drifts across the picture on most planets is off by default, with an option to bring it back.
- New: F9 switches between Enhanced Visuals and the game's own look, to compare.
